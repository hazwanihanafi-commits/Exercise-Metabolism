So where does the energy come from?

Muscle contraction uses ATP directly. Because the ATP pool is small, ATP must be continuously regenerated as exercise continues.

Food / stored fuel→ Substrates→ Energy pathways→ ATP resynthesis→ Muscle work

Important: the energy systems do not simply switch on one at a time. They all contribute, while their relative contribution changes with exercise demand.
