PART 4 • ELECTRON CARRIERS

What do NADH, FADH₂ and H⁺ actually do?

The TCA cycle generates NADH and FADH₂. These molecules carry high-energy electrons to the electron transport chain (ETC). The ETC uses electron flow to build an H⁺ (proton) gradient that helps drive ATP synthase.

04
IMPORTANT LANGUAGE
NADH is the standard abbreviation used here. FADH₂ is the reduced form of FAD. Both carry high-energy electrons to the respiratory chain. H⁺ means a proton; its gradient across the inner mitochondrial membrane provides the driving force for ATP synthase.

PART 5 • EXERCISE CONTEXT

Why does TCA activity matter during endurance exercise?

Sustained exercise places a large demand on oxidative ATP production. The TCA cycle supplies electron carriers that support that downstream process.

FINAL APPLICATION
Explain in your own words: What is the TCA cycle doing for a working athlete?
Model answer: The TCA cycle in the mitochondrial matrix processes acetyl-CoA and generates NADH and FADH₂. These electron carriers deliver high-energy electrons to the ETC, supporting the proton gradient and downstream oxidative ATP production needed during sustained exercise.
