04 • FINAL SYNTHESIS PROMPT

Without looking back: Explain in 4–6 sentences what happens from the moment exercise starts until recovery and longer-term adaptation.

Model structure: Exercise increases muscle work and ATP demand. Stored ATP is limited, so ATP is continuously resynthesised by overlapping ATP-PCr, glycolytic and oxidative pathways. The relative contribution depends on exercise intensity and duration. Cardiovascular, respiratory and metabolic responses support the increased demand. After exercise, recovery restores physiological balance and energy-related resources; repeated training can produce chronic adaptations that alter response and performance.
