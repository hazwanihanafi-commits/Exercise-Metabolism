SECTION 02 • YOUR LEARNING MAP

By the end, you should be able to explain the energy story.

Don't memorise isolated pathways. Follow the journey from food → fuel → ATP → exercise response, then use the evidence to explain what is happening.

01
🧭

Trace the pathway

Follow how nutrients become usable energy for exercise.

Student target: Connect digestion, absorption, cellular fuel, glycolysis, TCA and oxidative phosphorylation instead of viewing them as separate topics.
02
⚡

Explain ATP supply

Explain how ATP is regenerated as exercise demand changes.

Student target: Distinguish ATP-PCr, glycolytic and oxidative contributions and understand that they work together.
03
🔬

Interpret the evidence

Use physiological measurements to explain what is happening.

Student target: Connect variables such as intensity, duration, lactate, heart rate, VO₂ and RER to metabolic demand.
04
🏃

Apply to exercise

Predict metabolic demands across different exercise situations.

Student target: Compare sprinting, intervals, continuous exercise, recovery and prolonged exercise.
05
🩺

Think like an exercise scientist

Move from pathway knowledge to practical interpretation.

Student target: Use metabolic information to support exercise monitoring, case interpretation and exercise prescription.
06
🧠

Teach it back

Explain the energy story in your own words.

Student target: Build a concept map and explain why different pathways become more or less important during exercise.
