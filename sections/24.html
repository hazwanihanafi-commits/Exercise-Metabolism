04 • THE “WHY?” TEST

WHY FOOD?

Food provides substrates that can be transformed into usable cellular fuel.

WHY ATP?

ATP turnover must keep pace with the energy requirements of muscle work.

WHY RESPONSES?

Cardiovascular, respiratory, metabolic, muscle, thermoregulatory and blood-chemistry systems respond to the exercise demand.

WHY RECOVERY?

The body restores homeostasis and energy-related resources after the exercise demand falls.

WHY ADAPTATION?

Repeated training exposure can produce chronic adaptations.

WHY PRACTICAL APPLICATION?

The physiology informs assessment, exercise prescription, monitoring, recovery and performance decisions.

05 • DON'T JUST MEMORISE — BUILD THE FULL ANSWER

Exam / viva challenge: “Explain what happens when a person starts exercise and how repeated training can change their response.”

Use the structure below:

1
Demand
ATP demand rises with muscle work.
2
ATP supply
Multiple pathways resynthesise ATP.
3
Acute response
HR, ventilation, metabolism, muscle and other systems respond.
4
Recovery + adaptation
Restoration follows; repeated exposure can produce chronic adaptation.
Model answer: When exercise begins, muscle contraction increases ATP use and therefore ATP demand. Because stored ATP is limited, ATP must be continuously resynthesised through overlapping ATP-PCr, glycolytic and oxidative pathways, with their relative contributions changing according to intensity and duration. Cardiovascular, respiratory, metabolic, muscle, thermoregulatory and blood-chemistry responses help support the new demand. When exercise stops, recovery begins, including PCr resynthesis, lactate handling, restoration of physiological balance and longer-term restoration of energy stores. With repeated training exposure, chronic adaptations can develop and change physiological capacity and response to a given workload, supporting performance and practical exercise decisions.

06 • COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS — FINAL CHECK

❌ “Food becomes ATP in one step.”

Food-derived substrates pass through metabolic pathways before supporting ATP resynthesis.

❌ “One energy system is responsible for each exercise.”

The course model is overlapping pathways with changing relative contribution.

❌ “Recovery means everything instantly returns to baseline.”

Recovery processes occur on different time scales.

❌ “Training adaptation is just a bigger acute response.”

Chronic adaptation develops over repeated training exposure and represents longer-term change.
