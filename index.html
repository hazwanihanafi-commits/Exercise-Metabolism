Think Before You Continue

You ate a carbohydrate-rich meal two hours before exercise. You then start moderate-intensity exercise. What happens to the energy from your food?

Food is digested and absorbed, nutrients enter the circulation, and substrates can subsequently contribute to ATP production. During exercise, multiple energy pathways contribute simultaneously. Their relative contribution depends on factors such as exercise intensity, duration and substrate availability.
